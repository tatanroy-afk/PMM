Act I · Rebuilding Trust Deck slide 1

Five things are different this time.

Not a better pitch — a different operating model. Each change maps directly to one of the five points WOW raised.

Delivery

RISE with SAP

Structured, de-risked cloud transformation instead of a single big-bang commitment.

Method

SAP Activate

Milestone-gated methodology with value checkpoints — you see value before committing further.

Product

Business AI

Embedded, not bolted on — Joule and the Autonomous Enterprise run inside the process.

Commercial

cACV model

You pay for consumption, not licences purchased.

Incentive

Aligned KPIs

SAP's KPIs are tied to customer success, not deal size.

The change that matters most

SAP has skin in the game until the subscription ends. Under cACV, revenue only arrives when WOW actually uses — and benefits from — the platform.

That reverses the incentive that produced the last proposal. A licence sale rewards the size of the signature; a consumption model rewards adoption — which is the same thing WOW is trying to buy. It is also the direct fix for the licensing complexity WOW named: one meter, one number, visible monthly.

What this answers
  • Phasing replaces the all-at-once ask (point 01)
  • Milestone gates make delivery visible (point 02)
  • cACV replaces opaque licensing (points 03 & 04)
  • Outcome-linked KPIs replace deal-size KPIs (point 05)
